About us

More than just a coffee shop

A place where taste is born and every cup is a small work of art.

10 years

We roast beans by hand

ZERNARIA is a family coffee house founded in 2015. We work directly with farmers from Ethiopia, Colombia and Brazil, selecting only the best specialty-grade beans.

Every batch is roasted in small quantities in our own roastery so you can enjoy fresh coffee every day.
